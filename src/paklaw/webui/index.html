About

Answers come from BM25 retrieval over the consolidated federal statute text published by the Ministry of Law and Justice on the Pakistan Code (pakistancode.gov.pk). Every provision carries the date it came into force and, where it was repealed, substituted or omitted, the date it stopped. A question is answered only from provisions in force on the date you give; a cited provision that was not in force then is refused, not quoted.

It refuses rather than guesses: when nothing matches strongly enough, when a citation names no Act or an Act this corpus does not hold, or when the nearest provision is about a different offence. This is a research tool that quotes statute text; it is not legal advice. Runs on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere. Source: github.com/hammasbuilds/pak-law-assistant.
